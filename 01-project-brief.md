## 1. The question

Spatial distribution of selected public facilities across Kaduna North lga

## 2. Why this question

This question will provide information on how public facilities are located across the study area and will also show places that underserved in terms of basic social amenities across the lga for further action.

## 3. Study area

Kaduna North Local Government Area of Kaduna State Nigeria.Kaduna is one the 3 local government of Kaduna metropolis, it shares boundaries with Kaduna south, chikun and igabi lga area respectively

## 4. What I mean by the terms

"Underserved " this are places that have limited to none public facilities across the study area.
